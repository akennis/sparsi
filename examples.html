Examples

Six real workflows, explained

Each example is a self-contained Go program that demonstrates a distinct DAG pattern. Browse them to see how deterministic ops and AI ops combine in practice — including multi-model workflows where Claude and Gemini collaborate in a single graph.

01

Customer Support Ticket Triager

Classifies a free-text ticket with AI, routes it to a category-specific extraction lane, and emits a structured JSON brief. The clearest demonstration of the fan-out / predicate-gate / coalesce pattern.

ModeSelectOp predicate gates parallel lanes CoalesceNStringOp
→
02

Recipe Difficulty Analyzer

Runs three AI extractors in parallel over recipe instructions, then feeds counts and cook time into a deterministic scoring formula to classify difficulty and generate targeted cooking advice.

parallel extractors arithmetic scoring SliceLenOp difficulty lanes
→
03

GitHub README Quality Report

Fetches a repository README (trying main and master branches in parallel), fans out five AI probes concurrently, averages two scores deterministically, and appends an optional test-coverage warning.

parallel HTTP fetches five parallel probes SelectStringOp StringConcatOp
→
04

Weather-Aware Outfit Advisor

Parses live weather numbers with AI, classifies conditions with a multi-label op, computes temperature band and wet/windy flags deterministically, then generates outfit advice with an orthogonal extreme-weather probe.

AIParseNumberOp AIClassifyMultiLabelOp IfFloatGtOp orthogonal probe
→
05

HackerNews Topic Brief

Fans out per-story AI checks across N HN story titles using MapOver, filters and flattens results deterministically, picks a brief style from the dominant category, then summarises in technical, business, or policy format.

MapOver fan-out FilterAndFlattenOp DominantCategoryOp ModeSelectOp
→
06

Faithful Summary

Uses Claude to summarise a source document and Gemini to independently verify that every factual claim is grounded in the source. The clearest demonstration of the cross-model verification pattern — a second, independent model as a quality gate against hallucination.

Claude (summarize) Gemini (verify) FormatFaithfulnessCheckOp AIBoolOp
